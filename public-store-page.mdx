---
title: Public store page
description: Share a small store page with selected reusable payment links.
---

An operator can publish a store page at `{CHECKOUT_BASE_URL}/m/<handle>`.
It shows your store name, a short plain-text description and selected reusable
payment links. Each button opens the existing hosted [payment link](/payment-links)
flow. The page does not support buyer-entered amounts.

Only live-mode links explicitly selected for this page appear. An unpublished
page or deactivated store/company returns 404. Pages are excluded from search
indexing by default; indexing is a separate operator setting. Branding and
self-service editing in the merchant cabinet are follow-up features.

Give the operator a lowercase handle of 3–40 letters, digits or hyphens, a title
(up to 255 characters), description (up to 1000 characters), and the payment
links to display in order. Reserved system names cannot be handles. Review the
content before enabling publication: everything shown on this page is public.
Do not place personal data, credentials or private order details in the title,
description or selected payment-link descriptions.

The page supports English, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech and German. Clicking a link
rechecks its availability in checkout. Independently deactivated or exhausted
links may remain visible for up to 15 seconds; they cannot start an unavailable
payment. Confirm orders from your backend's verified [webhooks](/webhooks), never
from a buyer returning to your page.
