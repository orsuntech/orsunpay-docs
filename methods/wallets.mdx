For buyer-selected wallets, create a sale without a method and open its `paymentPageUrl`. Checkout can offer Apple Pay and Google Pay when your wallet connections are eligible. A provider may also offer a wallet inside its hosted card checkout based on your account settings.

## Apple Pay and Google Pay with Stripe

1. Enable the wallets on your Stripe account. If you connect with your own keys, enter the Stripe publishable key in the same mode as your secret or restricted key.
2. Check that the payment-method domain used for the wallet page is enabled on the Stripe account receiving the charge. Include `payment_intent.succeeded` and `payment_intent.payment_failed` in that account's webhook destination for OrsunPay.
3. Open the sale's hosted page on a device with an eligible wallet. Choosing the wallet opens a dedicated OrsunPay wallet page that loads Stripe Express Checkout. The buyer confirms in the wallet sheet.
4. Update the order from the signed OrsunPay webhook or a status read. If the buyer closes the wallet, check the transaction before offering a new sale. [Transaction status](/transaction-status)

If the option appears in checkout but Stripe shows no usable button, check the account, domain status, device, browser and wallet setup. Test with both a provisioned device and a browser without a usable wallet. Buyers should have another eligible option when you intend to accept alternatives.

## Other wallet connections

| Connection | What the buyer sees |
| --- | --- |
| Comgate or Ari10 Apple Pay / Google Pay | A provider redirect for the selected wallet. |
| Checkout.com card | The provider's hosted card page can also present wallets enabled on that account. Select its card connection when you want this flow. |
| Colibrix ONE | Apple Pay and Google Pay use hosted sessions. PayPal uses a separate local-method connection. These flows are sandbox-only. |

Keep provider keys on your backend or in the provider connection. The merchant API only needs the sale details and any permitted buyer fields. [Choose a payment method](/methods/choose-a-method)
