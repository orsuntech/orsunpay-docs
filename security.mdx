## Card data never reaches your server

OrsunPay's design keeps card numbers (PAN) and security codes (CVV) away from both your systems
and OrsunPay's servers:

- Card and wallet details are entered on the **provider's hosted page or the provider's hosted
  fields**, served from a separate origin from your site. Card numbers never touch OrsunPay servers.
- The merchant API **rejects raw card data**. A request containing a card number, CVV, track data
  or similar fields anywhere in its JSON fails with `400 CARD_DATA_NOT_ACCEPTED` before it is
  stored or logged. Do not try to send card details server-to-server.
- API responses and webhooks do not contain PAN or CVV. Keep only the identifiers you receive,
  such as transaction, refund and order IDs.

Your own page should only redirect to, or open, the URLs returned by the API (`paymentPageUrl`,
payment link `url`). The [`orsunpay-js` widget](/js-widget) only opens those URLs; it collects no
card data. The hosted payment page cannot be framed (`frame-ancestors 'none'`).

Your PCI DSS obligations depend on your integration and your acquirer. Confirm your validation
scope with your acquirer or assessor.

## Protect credentials

| Secret                 | Used for                       | Rules                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| API key (`test_`, `live_`) | Authenticating API requests | Server-side only; never in browsers, apps, URLs or logs. [Rotate](/authentication#rotation) on any suspicion |
| Webhook signing secret (`whsec_`) | Verifying webhooks  | Separate per mode; shown once; rotation is immediate               |
| Provider credentials   | Your PSP account               | Entered in the cabinet, encrypted at rest, never shown again in full |

Payment URLs (`paymentPageUrl`, link URLs) are bearer links: keep them out of analytics, logs and
public pages.

## Webhook and callback safety

Webhooks are signed with HMAC-SHA256 using your merchant's own secret; there is no shared platform
secret and no unsigned delivery. Callback URLs must be public HTTPS URLs; private, local and
reserved network addresses, URL credentials and redirects are rejected. See [webhooks](/webhooks).

## Cabinet access

The merchant cabinet uses per-user accounts, role permissions and MFA step-up for sensitive actions
such as key rotation, webhook secret rotation and payout approval. Sessions expire after inactivity.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to OrsunPay through your established private contact
channel. Do not open public issues, and do not include API keys, webhook secrets, card data or
personal information in a report. Do not test against live merchants or payments without
authorization.
